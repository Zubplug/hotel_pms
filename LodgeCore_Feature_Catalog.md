## 1. The LodgeCore Architecture
LodgeCore is built on a modular, eight-pillar architecture that allows hotels to start with the essentials and expand as they grow. Three principles define the platform:

**Offline-First:** Many cloud-first hotel platforms depend heavily on continuous internet connectivity. LodgeCore is engineered so that core hotel operations — check-in, check-out, POS billing, room charges, and housekeeping — continue without interruption on your local property network even during extended internet outages. All transactions automatically synchronize to the cloud when connectivity is restored.

**Unified Data:** Every module — from the restaurant POS to the general ledger — shares a single database. When a waiter posts a meal to a guest's room, the charge instantly appears on the folio, the inventory is automatically deducted, and the revenue is correctly classified in the departmental P&L report. No manual data entry, no spreadsheet transfers.

**Scalable Modular Design:** A 20-room boutique hotel and a 300-room group chain can both run on LodgeCore. The platform is edition-based and expansion-ready, so your software investment grows with your property.
