## 1. Executive Summary

African hospitality enterprises deserve a property management platform built for their realities — not adapted from markets that do not share their infrastructure, currency, regulatory, or operational challenges. LodgeCore Hospitality Technology was purpose-built to address precisely that gap.

LodgeCore is a fully integrated, cloud-native hotel operating system that unifies every department of a hospitality business — from front desk and housekeeping to finance, food & beverage, revenue management, and guest engagement — within a single, coherent platform. It is designed to operate reliably in environments where internet connectivity cannot be guaranteed, where FIRS tax compliance is non-negotiable, and where operational accountability across multiple properties must be maintained in real time.

This proposal presents LodgeCore's platform capabilities, commercial editions, and pricing structure. All pricing reflects our current Partner Programme rates, available to qualifying organisations during this period of strategic market expansion.

## 2. The LodgeCore Advantage

### Offline-First Architecture
LodgeCore's offline engine ensures uninterrupted hotel operations during network outages. Check-in, check-out, billing, room assignments, and point-of-sale transactions continue without disruption and synchronise automatically when connectivity is restored. This is not a fallback mode — it is the foundation of the platform.

### Unified Data, Unified Truth
Most hotels operate with fragmented systems — a PMS from one vendor, accounting from another, POS from a third. LodgeCore eliminates that fragmentation. Every transaction, guest interaction, inventory movement, and financial record flows through a single data layer, providing management with one version of the truth across every department and property.

### Built to Scale
LodgeCore serves independent hotels and large multi-property groups from the same platform. A property that begins on the Essential edition can graduate to Enterprise without migrating data, retraining staff, or changing vendors. Role-based access, multi-property consolidation, and open API integration ensure that LodgeCore grows with the business.

### Nigeria-Ready by Design
LodgeCore includes native support for FIRS VAT and levy compliance reporting, multi-currency billing, generator and fuel cost tracking, and naira-denominated financial reporting. These are not optional add-ons — they are core platform capabilities.

## 3. The 8 Platform Modules

LodgeCore is organised into eight integrated modules. All modules share a common data layer; there is no duplication, no reconciliation overhead, and no inter-system middleware to maintain.

---

### Module 1 — LodgeCore Core
*The operational heart of the platform.*

The Core module delivers the full property management suite: a configurable room reservation engine, interactive tape chart, individual and group guest folios, multi-currency billing, a compliant night audit engine, and LodgeCore's proprietary offline engine. Every guest-facing and front-desk workflow originates here.

**Includes:** PMS, Offline Engine, Tape Chart, Guest Folios, Night Audit, Multi-Currency Billing

---

### Module 2 — LodgeCore Operations
*Departmental coordination from a single console.*

LodgeCore Operations connects the operational departments — restaurant, housekeeping, maintenance, and procurement — to the same platform used by the front desk. Orders placed at the POS post directly to the guest folio. Housekeeping status updates are visible in real time on the tape chart. Maintenance requests are logged, assigned, and tracked to resolution.

**Includes:** Point of Sale (POS), Kitchen Display System (KDS), Housekeeping Management, Maintenance Tracking, Inventory Management, Procurement & Purchase Orders

---

### Module 3 — LodgeCore Commerce
*Direct revenue and distribution.*

The Commerce module enables hotels to drive direct bookings through a branded booking engine, apply rules-based pricing and availability policies, and manage room distribution. Channel Manager integration — connecting to OTAs including Booking.com and Expedia — is currently in active development and scheduled for release on the 2026 roadmap.

**Includes:** Direct Booking Engine, Booking Rules & Rate Management, Channel Manager *(Roadmap — Q4 2026)*

---

### Module 4 — LodgeCore Finance
*Institutional-grade hospitality accounting.*

LodgeCore Finance replaces the need for a standalone accounting system with a hospitality-native general ledger, city ledger, accounts payable, bank reconciliation, and departmental profit & loss reporting. Financial data flows automatically from every revenue-generating module, eliminating manual journal entries and reconciliation delays.

**Includes:** City Ledger, General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Bank Reconciliation, Departmental P&L Reporting, FIRS Tax Compliance Reports

---

### Module 5 — LodgeCore Guest
*Relationship-driven hospitality at scale.*

LodgeCore Guest enables hotels to build and leverage a rich database of guest history and preferences. Automated pre-arrival, in-stay, and post-departure communications are triggered by booking milestones. A tiered loyalty programme rewards repeat guests, and a dedicated guest mobile app delivers a digital check-in, service requests, and direct messaging capability.

**Includes:** 360° Guest Profiles, Automated Communications, VIP Loyalty Programme, Guest Mobile App

---

### Module 6 — LodgeCore Intelligence
*Revenue optimisation powered by AI.*

The Intelligence module applies machine learning to historical booking patterns, competitor rates, and market signals to generate dynamic pricing recommendations. An overbooking engine calculates optimal inventory release levels based on predicted no-show and cancellation rates, while demand forecasting tools support strategic planning across seasons.

**Includes:** AI Revenue Management, Overbooking Engine, Demand Forecasting

---

### Module 7 — LodgeCore Connected
*Smart hospitality for the modern guest.*

LodgeCore Connected brings together the digital guest touchpoints and sustainability tools of a contemporary hotel operation. Smart lock integration enables keyless room access managed from the PMS. A WhatsApp AI Concierge handles guest queries and service requests automatically. ESG reporting tracks energy and resource consumption, while a digital tipping feature allows guests to reward staff cashlessly.

**Includes:** Smart Lock Integration, ESG & Sustainability Reporting, WhatsApp AI Concierge, Digital Tipping

---

### Module 8 — LodgeCore Enterprise
*Multi-property governance and integration.*

LodgeCore Enterprise provides the infrastructure required by hotel groups, management companies, and ownership structures with multiple properties. Consolidated dashboards aggregate performance across all properties, role-based access control governs what each staff member can view and action, a full audit log records every system event, and an open API enables integration with third-party ERP, payroll, and business intelligence systems.

**Includes:** Multi-Property Management, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Full Audit Logs, Open API

## 10. Commercial Terms & Exclusions

### Payment Terms
- Cloud SaaS subscriptions are invoiced monthly in advance, or annually in advance for annual commitments.
- On-premise licences are payable 100% prior to deployment.
- Implementation fees are invoiced 50% upon engagement commencement and 50% upon go-live.
- All invoices are payable within 7 business days of issue.

### Inclusions
- All pricing includes access to LodgeCore's cloud infrastructure (for SaaS editions), standard software updates, and the stated support tier.
- The first 30 days of a new cloud subscription include complimentary assisted onboarding.

### Exclusions
The following items are not included in the subscription or licence fees and will be quoted separately where required:

- Hardware (tablets, receipt printers, kitchen display screens, smart lock hardware, biometric devices)
- Third-party software licences (e.g., SMS gateway credits, WhatsApp Business API fees, OTA channel manager fees pending roadmap release)
- Data migration from legacy systems beyond standard CSV import tooling
- Bespoke feature development or custom module engineering
- Travel and subsistence for on-site implementation engagements outside Lagos (quoted at cost)

### Partner Programme Terms
- Partner Programme rates are available under a signed Partner Programme agreement.
- Standard commercial rates apply immediately upon expiry or termination of the agreement.
- Partner Programme rates are non-transferable and may not be sub-licensed or resold.

### Pricing Validity
All pricing in this document is valid for 60 days from the date of issue. LodgeCore reserves the right to revise pricing at renewal, with a minimum of 60 days' advance notice to existing subscribers.
